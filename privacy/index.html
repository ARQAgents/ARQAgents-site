Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

ARQAgents ("we", "us") respects your privacy and handles personal data in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). This policy covers arqagents.com and our apps and tools.

1. What we collect

  • Contact details you send us, such as name, email, phone and message when you inquire or book a demo.
  • Messages you send to our chat assistants and business pages, so we can reply.
  • Technical data such as basic site usage and device information.

2. ARQAgents Poster and connected social accounts

ARQAgents Poster is a private tool used by the ARQAgents team to publish content to ARQAgents' own Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts. When the account owner connects an account, we store the access token needed to publish on their behalf, locally and securely, together with the posts they create. We use TikTok, Facebook and Instagram data only to publish content the account owner chose to publish. We do not collect personal data of other users of those platforms, do not sell it, and do not share it with third parties. The owner can disconnect at any time and revoke access in the platform's settings, after which we delete the stored token.

3. How we use data

To respond to inquiries, deliver and support our services, improve them, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run our services (for example hosting and messaging platforms), under appropriate safeguards, or where required by law.

5. Retention and security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above and apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No system is perfectly secure.

6. Your rights

Under RA 10173 you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its processing, or withdraw consent. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission.

7. Contact

Privacy requests and questions: hello@arqagents.com. See also our Terms of Service.
